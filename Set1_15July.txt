Q4: Demonstrate the difference between TRUNCATE, DELETE and DROP, and rename a table/column.
SQL> INSERT INTO Department VALUES(3,'Department of MM');

1 row created.

SQL> Commit;

Commit complete.

SQL> DELETE Department;

3 rows deleted.

SQL> ROLLBACK;

Rollback complete.

SQL> SELECT * FROM Department;

    DEPTID DEPTNAME
---------- --------------------------------------------------
         1 Department of MF
         2 Department of SF
         3 Department of MM
SQL> TRUNCATE TABLE Department;

Table truncated.

SQL> ROLLBACK;

Rollback complete.

SQL> SELECT * FROM Department;

no rows selected

SQL> RENAME Department TO Dept;

Table renamed.
